# Practitioner Signal

## Criteria (ordered by importance)

1. **PRACTITIONER SIGNAL** -- Does the author build things? Share first-hand experience? Show real-world knowledge from actually doing the work? This is the most important criterion.

2. **ORIGINAL THINKING** -- Is this the author's own insight or analysis, or a repost/link share/aggregation?

3. **ACTIONABLE VALUE** -- Can the reader learn something concrete and apply it to their own work?

4. **DEPTH** -- Does the content go beyond surface-level takes? Does it show nuance, tradeoffs, or non-obvious conclusions?

5. **NOVELTY** -- Is this something few people are discussing? Does it surface a non-obvious connection or an emerging signal before mainstream?

6. **CROSS-DOMAIN** -- Does the content bridge multiple fields? Biology+CS, art+engineering, philosophy+ML, hardware+software combinations score higher.

## Tiebreaker
Force a strict ordering. Use NOVELTY and CROSS-DOMAIN as secondary differentiators. Prefer items with more original thinking.

## Context
General-purpose content quality rubric. Designed to surface practitioner-grade technical content. Penalizes: news aggregation, listicles, funding announcements, product launches without depth, corporate PR. Rewards: field reports, architecture decisions, debugging war stories, research findings, cross-domain synthesis.
