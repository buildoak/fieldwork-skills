# Signal-Serendipity-Entropy

## Criteria (ordered by importance)

1. **HIGH SIGNAL** -- Practitioner builds, first-hand experience, proven depth, operational knowledge. Does the author build things? Share real-world knowledge from actually doing the work?

2. **HIGH SERENDIPITY** -- Surprising connections, "why does nobody talk about this?", non-obvious angles, emerging signals before mainstream. Does this surface something few people are discussing?

3. **HIGH ENTROPY** -- Information-dense, unusual combinations, far from consensus, cross-domain bridging. Does this combine ideas from different fields in unexpected ways?

## Tiebreaker
Prefer items with more original thinking. Between equal items, favor the one that bridges more domains or surfaces a less-discussed angle.

## Context
Designed for content curation across diverse sources. Rewards practitioner depth and intellectual surprise over polish or popularity. News aggregation, hot takes, and corporate PR score low. Deep dives, field reports, and cross-domain synthesis score high.
